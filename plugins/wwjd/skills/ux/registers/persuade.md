# Persuade: landing, pricing, campaign, waitlist

Profile: `data-profile="marketing"`, primary width 390. Colour: identity; a committed ground and fields of colour are fine, the accent still marks the action.

Success: the visitor decides and acts. A persuade page argues one claim and earns one action. Layouts: `landing`, `pricing`.

## Method
1. **Inventory** 8–12 facts only this subject has (name and meaning, place, real numbers, materials, rituals, artefacts, real imagery). Vague brief: state your reading in one line and proceed.
2. **Argument:** each section is one claim plus its proof; no claim, cut. The order of claims is the page order, never the template (hero, logos, 3 features, testimonial, pricing, CTA).
3. **Idea sentence:** "On this page [thing from the inventory] becomes [device]." **Competitor test:** swap in the nearest rival; if it still works, it's a category page. **Self-contradiction test:** the page demonstrates every hero claim (a "simple" product whose page takes 10s to parse fails).
4. **Copy deck before markup.** The first screen answers the visitor's first question in large type with one real button. Write the full content; thin sections lose.
5. **One signature move;** the rest quiet. Before shipping, remove one accessory.

## The page converts; the journey lives elsewhere
- Ordering, booking or signing up happen on their own stepped pages, each step in its register (product list in Transact → delivery choice in Flow → checkout); never a shop inside the landing page.

## Composition
- **One dominant thing per screen;** neighbouring sections never share a layout.
- **Above the fold at 1280×800:** headline, a lede of ≤2 lines, the CTA.
- **The hero may be the page's one centred moment:** ≤2 elements, the CTA off-axis.
- **Hero composition serves the idea sentence** (`references/heroes.md`); left text / right image is never the default.
- **A hero tool carries its own CTA** (picker, estimator, calculator), and its input carries into the order or signup flow. Never a "try it" widget here and a "do it" button elsewhere.
- **Grade every fact** (`references/budgets.md`): the one that settles or delights gets its own moment with a small visual; the deciding fact sits next to the action.
- **Carry the idea** through pricing, FAQ and footer. Answer objections in the argument.
- **Nav** is a plain bar: wordmark, 3–5 links, one action; a page inside a larger site keeps its nav. **Footer** is a designed close (the name at full width, or one quiet line); a site's footer groups the links people look for there, never a link farm.

## Type
- **Headlines ≤2 lines at every width** (~32–50 characters). Fix in order: cut words, widen the measure, shrink the size.

## Imagery
- **Real raster first:** the subject's own, then generated, then open collections, then honest stock. One crop ratio, one grade.
- **Software shows its real UI,** rendered from the product; never drawn browser chrome or phone frames.

## Proof and numbers
- **Real proof only** (Floor); a proof number must change the decision, never a strip of round numbers.
- **Let them try it:** for a price or risk decision, a calculator or live preview instead of a description.
- **B2B:** give the champion copy they can paste into an internal memo.

## Motion
`wwjd:motion` skill. Never hijack scrolling.

## Performance and meta
- **Performance is design:** preload the LCP image, ≤2 font files above the fold, no shift as the hero loads (`rules/visual.md` Type).
- **Meta designed with the page:** a specific `<title>`, description, OG image, favicon, 404.
