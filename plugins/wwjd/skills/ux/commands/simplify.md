# simplify: remove, then reorder

For "too busy", "simplify", "clean up", "I got overloaded". Delete, then re-rank what stays so the eye lands on the right thing first. Sweep the whole scope; fix each pattern at its source (the kit component, not each call site).

## 1. Delete
- **Explaining text:** intros, "how this works", helper text restating the label, legends repeating the axis, headers over self-evident things, implementation commentary in UI or mock data.
- **Reassurance and narration:** "Nothing is ever lost", "When you click X we will…".
- **Duplicates:** a second control for one job; the same status twice ("Draft" + "Unpublished"); two paths to one place; the same avatar twice in a view.
- **Nothing-facts** (`references/budgets.md` grades): system facts nobody acts on, counts that change no decision, zero counts. Big counts become a dot or go.
- **Unneeded controls:** toggles, filters, sort and search the task doesn't need; short lists need none.
- **Machinery receipts:** steps run, files read, "AI is thinking…" once the result shows.
- **Over budget:** grade first (`budgets.md` § Grade gate). Budget surfaces drop the "nothing" items and move the rest one step away; exposure surfaces only organise.

## 2. Reduce
- **Status:** many statuses boil down to the one or two that need a person; the rest stays calm.
- **Choices:** default instead of asking; drop steps, confirmations and choices with an obvious answer.
- **Disclosure:** rare actions and detail go one layer down (`rules/actions.md` § Disclosure).
- **Structure:** a card, border or section that holds one thing goes.
- **Labels:** one concept per label; a label fusing two backend concepts ("Ledger · Payout") is split or flattened.

## 3. Reorder by attention
- **What needs the person leads;** what's fine goes quiet or lower. Group by the real logic, ≤ ~7 ungrouped items per section.
- **Re-rank the actions per region** (`rules/actions.md` § Hierarchy per region): one primary (the next step), one secondary, the rest one step away; no entry point gets more space than its use earns.
- **The fact that settles the decision gets its own moment.**

## Not this
- **Keep** what the Floor keeps (SKILL.md § 2 Honesty).
- **Thin is not clean:** Persuade and Editorial keep their full content; fewer elements, stronger hierarchy.
- **Freed space** closes up or lets what remains grow; never ornament.
- **Who loses the control?** Power users, keyboard and assistive-tech users, automation (selectors, deep links, extensions). If the habit wouldn't come back when re-added, the removal is one-way: say so.

**Done when** every surface is within its `budgets.md` row, each region has one primary, and the squint test (`references/bans.md` § Quick tests) passes.
