# Persuade: landing, pricing, campaign, waitlist

Success: the visitor decides and acts. A persuade page argues one claim and earns one action.

## Method
1. **Inventory** 8–12 facts that only this subject has: name and meaning, place, real numbers, materials, rituals, artefacts, imagery that exists. If the brief is vague, state your reading in one line and proceed.
2. **Argument:** each section is one claim plus its proof. A section with no claim is cut. The order of claims is the page order, which kills the template: hero, logos, 3 features, testimonial, pricing, CTA.
3. **Idea sentence:** "On this page [thing from the inventory] becomes [device]." Then the competitor test: swap in the nearest rival. If the page still works, it is a category page, not this subject's.
4. **Copy deck before markup.** The first screen answers the visitor's first question in large type, with one real button. Write the full content: thin sections are the most common reason a clean page loses.
5. **Spend boldness in one place.** One memorable element; everything else quiet. Before shipping, remove one accessory.

## Composition
- **One dominant thing per screen.** Neighbouring sections never share a layout.
- **Spacing:** the gap between sections is larger than any gap inside one.
- **Above the fold at 1280×800:** the headline, a lede of at most 2 lines and the CTA.
- **Alignment:** start-aligned by default. The hero may be the page's one centred moment, at most 2 elements, with the CTA placed off-axis.
- **Carry the idea through** pricing, FAQ and footer, not just the hero.
- **Nav and footer:**
  - Nav is a plain bar: wordmark, 3–5 links, one action.
  - The footer is a designed close (the name at full width, or one quiet line), not a link farm.
- **Mobile is first-class:** most landing traffic is mobile. Compose 390 on its own; don't just stack desktop.

## Type and colour
- **Headlines:** at most 2 lines at every width (about 32–50 characters). Fix in this order: cut words, widen the measure, shrink the size.
- **Type scale:** fluid `clamp()` display type, display at least 3× body, body 17–20px. Use the project's faces; at most a display face plus a text face.
- **Colour is identity here.** A committed ground and fields of colour are allowed. The accent still marks the action.
- **Gradients:** flat by default. Use one only when the project's look owns it, never as decoration.

## Imagery
- **Source:** real raster first (the subject's own, then generated, then open collections, then honest stock). One crop ratio and one grade.
- **Software shows its real UI,** rendered from the product, never drawn browser chrome or phone frames.
- **Text over media** stays legible with a scrim.

## Proof and numbers
- **Real proof only:** logos, quotes, counts. If it is not confirmed, flag it as a sample or cut it.
- **Numbers here are content.** A real proof number changes the decision; a stat strip of round numbers does not.

## Motion
- **One orchestrated moment**, plus scroll-linked motion only where it carries the idea.
- **Never:** scroll hijack, stagger-in on every block, bouncing chevrons, custom cursors.
- **Interruption** fades cleanly and never locks clicks.

## Performance and meta
- **Performance is design:** preload the LCP image, at most 2 font files above the fold, metric-matched font fallbacks, no layout shift as the hero loads.
- **Meta surfaces are designed with the page:** title, description, OG image, favicon, 404.

## Tells to avoid
- A centred headline over 3 icon cards.
- An eyebrow kicker above the H1.
- A gradient word in the headline.
- Bento by default.
- A fake logo strip; "Trusted by 10,000+ teams".
- A stat banner of round numbers.
- Fade-up on everything.
- A FAQ accordion standing in for answers.
- A link-farm footer.
